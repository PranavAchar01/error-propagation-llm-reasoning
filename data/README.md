## Generation parameters (PrOntoQA)

Generated by the authors' own `run_experiment.py --model-name json`, at hop
counts 1–5 for seeds 1, 2, 3, 120 trials each.

`--ontology fictional` is deliberate. Fictional predicates (`zumpus`, `wumpus`)
make world knowledge useless, so a correct answer has to come through the
premises — the necessary condition for a propagation measurement to mean
anything. It also means the items cannot appear in any pretraining corpus, which
neutralises contamination for the primary result.

## Known quirks, handled explicitly in `src/epr/datasets.py`

- **FOLIO label drift.** The train split calls the third class `Unknown`; the
  validation split calls it `Uncertain`. The splits also differ in columns
  (train has no `conclusion-FOL`; validation has no `example_id`/`story_id`).
  Everything is normalised to `{True, False, Unknown}`.
- **ProofWriter mixed types.** Answers are Python booleans `True`/`False` and
  the *string* `"Unknown"`.
- **ProofWriter depth tail.** The `depth-5` split contains 10 items at QDep 6–7
  out of 20,030. Excluded from slope fits; the exclusion is logged.
- **Negation as failure.** ~3.5% of ProofWriter theories use the `~` polarity
  marker, which asserts *unprovability*. That is not decidable by checking a step
  against its cited premises, so those theories are excluded and counted rather
  than approximated. See `UnsupportedTheory` in `src/epr/parsers.py`.
- **Open-world `Unknown` items have no proof** by construction, so they carry
  answer accuracy but contribute no step-level metrics.
